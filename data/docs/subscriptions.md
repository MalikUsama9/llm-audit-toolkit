# BrewNest Subscription Policy

## Overview

BrewNest subscriptions deliver fresh coffee on a schedule you choose. There is no minimum commitment and no cancellation fee.

## Plans

- **Delivery frequency:** every 1 week, 2 weeks, or 4 weeks
- **Bag sizes:** 12 oz or 2 lb
- **Discount:** 10% off the listed price on every shipment
- **Shipping:** Always free with Standard shipping

Gift cards cannot be used to pay for subscriptions.

## Billing

Your card is charged on the day each order ships. If a payment fails, we retry after 2 days and again after 5 days. If both retries fail, the subscription is paused and we email you.

## Changing a Subscription

Log in and go to "My Subscriptions". You can:

- Change the coffee, bag size, or grind
- Change the delivery frequency
- Change the shipping address
- Skip a single delivery
- Pause for up to 3 months
- Cancel at any time

All changes must be made at least 48 hours before your next shipment date. Changes made later apply to the following shipment.

## Skipping and Pausing

Skipping affects only one delivery and your schedule continues as normal. Pausing stops all deliveries for 1 to 3 months. After 3 months of pause, the subscription resumes automatically unless you cancel.

## Cancelling

Cancel anytime from "My Subscriptions". If your next order has already been roasted, it will still ship and be charged. After cancelling, you can reactivate within 30 days and keep your saved settings.

## Subscription Problems

If a subscription order arrives late, damaged, or wrong, contact support within 7 days of delivery. The Fresh Promise from our returns policy applies to subscription bags too.

## Subscription Rewards

After your 6th subscription shipment, you get a free Paper Filters pack (100 count) with your next order. After your 12th shipment, you get a free 4 oz sample bag of a new coffee. Rewards are applied automatically.

## Discount Rules

The subscription discount cannot be combined with the student and military discount. It can be combined with the WELCOME15 code on the first order only.
