# Support and Contact

## Contact details
- Email: support@tidewellhome.example
- Phone: +1 (555) 010-0142
- Live chat: on the website, bottom-right corner
- Safety issues: safety@tidewellhome.example

## Support hours
Live chat and email support are open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM PT, and Saturday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM PT. Phone support is shorter: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM PT, with no phone support on Saturday. We are closed on Sundays.

## Holiday closures
We are closed on New Year's Day (January 1), Independence Day (July 4), Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day (December 25). Messages sent on a closure day are answered on the next business day.

## Response times
We reply to email within 1 business day. Live chat has an average wait of about 3 minutes during support hours. After hours, you can leave a chat message and we answer it the next time we open.

## What to include in a message
Please include your order number and the email address used at checkout. For damaged or defective items, attach photos. Do not send your full card number, your password or a gift card code in an email or chat. Our team will never ask for your password.

## Talking to a human
If the assistant cannot help or you prefer a person, ask for a human in live chat or email support. A person will take over during support hours. Complaints about injuries, allergic reactions or unsafe products should go straight to safety@tidewellhome.example, and we reply to those within 4 business hours.

## Escalation
If you are not happy with an answer, ask for a supervisor. A supervisor reviews the case and replies within 2 business days.

## Legal and press requests
Legal notices and press questions are not handled by the support team. Send them by email to legal@tidewellhome.example.

## Languages
Support is available in English. We do not currently offer support in other languages.

## Accessibility
If you have trouble using our website, tell us and we will help you place your order by phone or email.
