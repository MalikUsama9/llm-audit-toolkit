# Kitchen Products

## Tidewell Enameled Dutch Oven (5.5 qt)
The Dutch Oven is enameled cast iron and weighs 12.4 lb. It comes in three colors: Sea Glass, Oat and Charcoal. It is oven safe to 500°F (260°C). The lid knob is rated only to 450°F (232°C), so use the lid at lower temperatures or swap the knob. It works on gas, electric, ceramic and induction cooktops. We recommend hand washing. It is dishwasher safe, but repeated dishwasher cycles can dull the enamel over time.

## Dutch Oven care
Use wooden or silicone utensils. Metal utensils can scratch the enamel. Do not heat the empty pot on high heat, and do not put a hot pot into cold water, because sudden temperature change can crack the enamel. Soak stuck-on food in warm water and use a nylon scrubber. Do not use steel wool.

## Pinebrook Knife Set (6 pieces)
The set includes an 8-inch chef's knife, an 8-inch bread knife, a 6-inch utility knife, a 3.5-inch paring knife, kitchen shears and a bamboo block. The blades are high-carbon stainless steel. Knives must be hand washed only. A dishwasher can dull the edge and damage the handles. Dry the knives right after washing. We suggest honing them every few weeks and professionally sharpening them about every 6 months.

## Everyday Stoneware Dinner Set (16 pieces)
The set serves four and has four dinner plates, four salad plates, four bowls and four mugs. It is microwave safe and dishwasher safe. It is not oven safe, broiler safe or stovetop safe. Stoneware can chip if knocked against hard surfaces, and chips from impact are not covered by the warranty.

## Glass Storage Containers (10 pieces)
The set has five containers and five lids. The glass containers are safe for the oven up to 425°F (218°C), the freezer and the dishwasher. The lids are BPA-free plastic. The lids are not oven safe, so remove them before heating the glass in the oven. The lids can go in the top rack of the dishwasher. Do not move hot glass straight into the freezer or cold glass straight into a hot oven.

## Kitchen product weight and size notes
The Dutch Oven is a 5.5 qt pot with a 10.5-inch diameter. The Pinebrook knife block is 9 inches tall. Dinner plates are 10.5 inches across, and mugs hold 12 oz.
