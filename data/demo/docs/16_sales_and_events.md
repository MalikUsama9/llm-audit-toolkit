# Sales and Seasonal Events

## How our sales work
Sale prices are shown on the product page and applied automatically. Sale items follow our normal return policy unless they are labeled Final Sale. Sales end at 11:59 PM PT on the last day shown.

## Black Friday to Cyber Monday
The Black Friday to Cyber Monday sale starts on the Wednesday before Thanksgiving at 12:00 AM PT and ends on Cyber Monday at 11:59 PM PT. During this sale, price adjustments are not available. Items bought during the sale can still be returned within the normal return window, and if they are delivered between November 1 and December 31, the extended holiday return deadline of January 31 applies.

## Early access for Club members
Tidewell Club members get early access to every named sale, 24 hours before the public. Early access starts at 12:00 AM PT, 24 hours before the sale opens to everyone.

## Named sales during the year
- **Spring Refresh:** the first two weeks of March, with discounts on bedding and bath.
- **Summer Outdoor Sale:** June 1 to June 21, with discounts on outdoor products.
- **Fall Kitchen Event:** the last two weeks of September, with discounts on kitchen products.
These dates are our usual schedule, not a promise, and they can change.

## Sale items that sell out
We cannot hold sale items or offer rain checks. When a sale item sells out, it is gone for the rest of the sale. Use Notify Me on the product page to get an email if it returns.

## Free gift offers
From time to time we offer a free item with an order over a certain amount. The terms are shown on the offer page. A free gift is removed from the order if you return the qualifying items so that the order drops below the minimum.

## Promo codes during sales
You can combine a promo code with a sale price unless the sale page says otherwise. The code WELCOME10 still needs a $30 minimum before tax and still does not apply to Final Sale items.

## Free shipping during sales
The normal free-shipping threshold of $49 applies during sales. It is measured after discounts, so a discounted order might fall below the threshold.

## Shipping delays during peak season
From November 1 to December 24, processing can take up to 2 extra business days, even for orders placed during a sale. See the shipping policy for the last order dates for Christmas delivery.

## Sale emails
Sale announcements are marketing emails. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in the email.
