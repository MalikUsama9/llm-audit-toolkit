# About Tidewell Home

*Tidewell Home is a fictional company created for software testing. Every name, price, policy and date in these documents is invented.*

## Who we are
Tidewell Home is an online shop for home goods. It was founded in 2019 by siblings Rena and Colby Vance. Our head office is in Portland, Oregon, and every order ships from our fulfillment center in Reno, Nevada. We sell online only. We have no physical stores, so in-store pickup is not available.

## What we sell
We sell about 140 products in five categories: Kitchen, Bedding, Bath, Storage and Outdoor. Our best-known products are the Tidewell Enameled Dutch Oven, the Linen Sheet Set, the Turkish Cotton Towel Set and the Cedar Chest. A full price list is in the product catalog document.

## Prices and time zone
All prices on our website are in US dollars (USD). Every cutoff time, support hour and deadline in our policies uses Pacific Time (PT), which means Pacific Standard Time in winter and Pacific Daylight Time in summer.

## Packaging
Since March 1, 2023, every order ships in 100% recycled cardboard, with no plastic mailers and no plastic fill. Orders that contain glassware or stoneware use molded paper-pulp cushioning instead of crinkle paper. The packing slip is printed on recycled paper. If an order is marked as a gift, the packing slip hides all prices.

## Shipping emissions
We offset the carbon emissions of all Standard shipments through a verified offset partner. Expedited and Overnight shipments are not offset. The offset is paid by Tidewell Home and never added to your order total.
