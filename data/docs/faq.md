# BrewNest Frequently Asked Questions

## About BrewNest

**What is BrewNest?**
BrewNest is an online store that sells freshly roasted coffee beans, brewing equipment, and coffee subscriptions. We were founded in 2019 in Portland, Oregon, and ship across the United States and Canada.

**Where are your beans roasted?**
All beans are roasted in small batches at our roastery at 214 Alder Street, Portland, Oregon. We roast every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

**How fresh are the beans when I receive them?**
Orders are roasted to order and shipped within 24 hours of roasting. Most customers receive their beans 3 to 6 days after the roast date. The roast date is printed on every bag.

## Orders and Accounts

**Do I need an account to place an order?**
No. You can check out as a guest. However, an account lets you track orders, save addresses, and manage subscriptions.

**How do I reset my password?**
Click "Sign In", then "Forgot password". Enter your email and we will send a reset link that expires after 60 minutes.

**Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?**
You can change or cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it by emailing support@brewnest.example. After 2 hours, the order enters roasting and cannot be changed.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept checks or cash on delivery.

**Is my payment information secure?**
Yes. Payments are processed by a PCI-compliant provider. BrewNest never stores your full card number.

## Products

**What roast levels do you offer?**
We offer three roast levels: Light, Medium, and Dark. Each product page shows a roast scale from 1 (lightest) to 5 (darkest).

**Do you sell decaf?**
Yes. Our Swiss Water Decaf is available in 12 oz and 2 lb bags. It is decaffeinated using a chemical-free process that removes 99.9% of caffeine.

**Are your beans organic?**
Four of our eight single-origin beans are USDA Organic certified. Look for the green "Organic" badge on the product page.

**Do you sell ground coffee?**
Yes. At checkout you can choose Whole Bean, Coarse, Medium, or Fine grind at no extra cost.

**How should I store my coffee?**
Keep beans in an airtight container away from light, heat, and moisture. Do not store them in the refrigerator or freezer. For best flavor, use beans within 4 weeks of the roast date.

## Subscriptions

**How do subscriptions work?**
Choose a coffee, a bag size, and a delivery frequency (every 1, 2, or 4 weeks). Your card is charged on the day each order ships.

**Do subscribers get a discount?**
Yes. Subscribers save 10% on every order and get free shipping on all subscription deliveries.

**How do I pause or cancel my subscription?**
Log in, go to "My Subscriptions", and choose Pause or Cancel. You can pause for up to 3 months. Changes must be made at least 48 hours before your next shipment date.
