# BrewNest Equipment Warranty

## Coverage

BrewNest brand equipment (products with "Nest" in the name) includes a 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date. It covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.

## What Is Covered

- Electric grinder motor failure under normal use
- Kettle heating element failure
- Espresso machine pump or pressure failure
- Scale display or sensor defects
- Cracks in ceramic drippers that were present on arrival

## What Is Not Covered

- Damage from drops, misuse, or accidents
- Normal wear, including worn burrs, scratched surfaces, and used gaskets
- Damage from using the wrong voltage or an unapproved power adapter
- Descaling failures caused by not cleaning the machine
- Third-party brands, which follow the manufacturer's own warranty

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

1. Email support@brewnest.example with your order number, a description of the problem, and a short video or photos.
2. Our team replies within 1 business day with troubleshooting steps.
3. If the issue cannot be fixed, we send a prepaid return label.
4. After we receive and inspect the item, we send a replacement or issue a refund within 5 business days.

## Replacement Parts

Replacement burrs for the Nest Burr Grinder cost $18.00 and for the Nest Burr Grinder Pro cost $34.00. Replacement filters, gaskets, and glass carafes are available in the equipment section. Replacement parts carry a 90-day warranty.

## Extended Warranty

We do not currently offer extended warranty plans.

## Warranty Transfer

The warranty applies to the original purchaser only and cannot be transferred to another owner. Gift recipients are covered if the gift was shipped directly from BrewNest.
