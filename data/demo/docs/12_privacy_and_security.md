# Privacy and Security

## What data we collect
We collect your name, email address, shipping and billing addresses, order history and the device information that your browser sends us. We also collect any message you send to support. We use this to process orders, prevent fraud and improve the website.

## Payment card data
Tidewell Home does not store your full card number. Card payments are processed by our payment partner, and we only see the card type and the last 4 digits.

## We do not sell your data
We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We share data only with the carriers who deliver your order, our payment partner, our email service and our fraud-check provider, and only what each needs to do its job.

## Marketing emails
We send marketing emails only to people who agreed to receive them. Every email has an unsubscribe link. After you unsubscribe it can take up to 10 business days for the change to apply to all our systems. Order and shipping emails are not marketing emails, and you will still receive them.

## Cookies
We use cookies to keep items in your cart, remember your login and measure website traffic. You can turn off non-essential cookies in your browser or in the cookie banner. The cart will not work without essential cookies.

## Data retention
We keep order records for 7 years, as required for tax purposes. Support messages are kept for 3 years. Marketing preferences are kept until you change them.

## Access and deletion requests
You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@tidewellhome.example. We respond within 30 days. Some records, such as order records needed for tax, cannot be deleted before 7 years.

## Children
Our website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child gave us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Account security
Use a strong, unique password. A password reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If you see an order you did not place, contact support right away and change your password. Our team will never call you to ask for your password or a gift card code.

## Changes to this policy
When we change this policy in a meaningful way, we post the new version on the website and email account holders at least 14 days before it takes effect.
