## Subscriptions

Subscribers save 10% off the listed price on every shipment and ship free. Example: a 12 oz bag of Colombia Huila normally costs $15.00, so the subscription price is $13.50.

Available frequencies: every 1 week, 2 weeks, or 4 weeks. There is no minimum commitment and no cancellation fee.

## Taxes

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address. Coffee is tax-exempt in most US states, while equipment is taxable where required by law.

## Price Matching

We do not offer price matching. If a price drops within 7 days of your purchase, contact support and we will refund the difference once per order.
