CATEGORY: multi_hop

QUESTION: I am a Tidewell Club member living in Alaska. If I order a heavy item, will I have to pay for shipping?

REFERENCE: Yes, you will have to pay the $19.95 heavy item surcharge. While Tidewell Club members receive free Standard shipping with no minimum, the FAQ states that this surcharge applies to heavy items and is not removed by free shipping offers.

SOURCE_REF: ["13_faq.md#where-is-my-order", "02_shipping_policy.md#free-shipping"]

SOURCE_TEXT: Frequently Asked Questions > Where is my order?

Open your account and go to Orders, or use the tracking link in your shipping email. If tracking has not moved for 7 business days, contact support.

Frequently Asked Questions > Do you ship internationally?
We ship to the contiguous US, Alaska, Hawaii and Canada only. Shipping times and prices for each are in the shipping policy.

Frequently Asked Questions > What is the cheapest way to get free shipping?
Spend $49 or more in the contiguous US for free Standard shipping. Alaska and Hawaii need $79. Tidewell Club members get free Standard shipping with no minimum.

Frequently Asked Questions > Can I return something I bought on sale?
Yes, if it is not marked Final Sale and you return it within 30 days of delivery. Final Sale items cannot be returned.

Frequently Asked Questions > How do I exchange an item for a different size?
Start a return for the original item and place a new order for the size you want. If you are exchanging for a different size or color of the same item, there is no return label fee.

Frequently Asked Questions > Why was I charged shipping on my large item even though my order was over $49?
Heavy items carry a $19.95 surcharge that free shipping does not remove.

Frequently Asked Questions > Can I use two promo codes?
No. One promo code per order.

Frequently Asked Questions > Is the Dutch Oven dishwasher safe?
Yes, but we recommend hand washing, because repeated dishwasher cycles can dull the enamel.

Frequently Asked Questions > Can I put the Pinebrook knives in the dishwasher?
No. The knives must be hand washed only.

Frequently Asked Questions > How do I use my store credit?
It is applied automatically at checkout and does not expire.

Frequently Asked Questions > Do you offer gift wrapping?
Please see our gift message options in the orders document.

---

Shipping Policy > Free shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders of $49 or more to the contiguous US. The $49 is measured after discounts and promo codes are applied and before tax. Free shipping applies to Standard only. If you choose Expedited or Overnight on a qualifying order, you pay the full price of that option. Tidewell Club members get free Standard shipping on all US orders with no minimum.

Shipping Policy > Alaska and Hawaii
Orders to Alaska and Hawaii ship Standard only. Delivery takes 8 to 12 business days and costs $9.95. Free shipping starts at $79 for these two states.

Shipping Policy > Canada
We ship to Canada only. Delivery takes 8 to 14 business days and costs a flat $14.95. There is no free-shipping threshold for Canada. Canadian customers pay any import duties and taxes charged at the border. We do not ship to any other country.
