# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The 30 days start on the day the carrier marks the package delivered, not the day you placed the order. Items delivered between November 1 and December 31 can be returned until January 31.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unused and in their original packaging. We may refuse or partly refund an item that shows signs of use, washing or damage that did not come from shipping.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Personalized or engraved items.
- Gift cards.
- Opened soaps, bath bombs and bath salts, for hygiene reasons. Unopened ones can be returned.
- Items marked Final Sale on the product page.

## How to start a return
Request a return in your account under Orders. You will receive a return authorization number (RMA). The RMA is valid for 14 days. We cannot accept a return without an RMA. Print the prepaid label and drop the package at any UPS or USPS location.

## Return shipping cost
A flat $6.50 label fee is deducted from your refund. There is no label fee in these cases: the item is defective, we sent the wrong item, or you are exchanging for a different size or color of the same item. Customers in Canada pay their own return shipping and we do not provide a prepaid label.

## Restocking fee on large items
Returning a heavy item (an item over 40 lb) costs a 15% restocking fee, taken from the item price. The fee is not charged when the item is defective or we sent the wrong item.

## When you get your refund
We process your refund within 5 business days after our warehouse receives the return. Your bank may then take another 3 to 10 business days to show it. Refunds go back to the original payment method. The original shipping cost is refunded only if the return is our error. Otherwise shipping is not refunded.

## Store credit option
You can choose store credit instead of a refund to your card. Store credit comes with a 10% bonus. For example, a $50 return becomes $55 in store credit. Store credit never expires.

## Defective items
If an item arrives defective, report it within 90 days of delivery and include photos. You can choose a replacement or a refund. If the replacement is out of stock, we offer a similar item or a refund, whichever you prefer.

## Items damaged in transit
Report shipping damage within 7 days of delivery and include photos of the item and the box. We send a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

## Wrong item received
Tell us within 14 days of delivery. We send the right item and pay for the return of the wrong one.
