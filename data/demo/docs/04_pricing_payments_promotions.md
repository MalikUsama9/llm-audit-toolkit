# Pricing, Payments and Promotions

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Tidewell gift cards and Tidewell store credit. We do not accept cash on delivery, checks, money orders or cryptocurrency. We do not offer buy-now-pay-later plans or monthly installment payments at this time.

## When you are charged
Your card is charged when you place the order. The only exception is pre-order items, which are charged on the day they ship. If your card is declined, the order is not placed and no hold stays on your account.

## Paying with two methods
You can split one order across up to 2 payment methods, for example store credit plus one card. Gift cards and store credit count as payment methods.

## Sales tax
We collect sales tax in the states where we are required to, based on the shipping address. Tax is shown at checkout before you pay. Canadian orders are not charged US sales tax, but duties and taxes apply at the border (see the shipping policy).

## Currency
All prices and charges are in US dollars, including orders to Canada. Your bank may add a currency conversion fee, which we do not control.

## Promo codes
You can use one promo code per order. Promo codes cannot be combined with each other. They can be combined with Tidewell Club benefits. The code WELCOME10 gives 10% off the first order of a new customer. It needs a merchandise subtotal of at least $30 before tax and does not apply to gift cards or Final Sale items. WELCOME10 can be used once per customer.

## Price adjustments
If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of your purchase date, contact support and we refund the difference to your original payment method within 5 business days. A price adjustment can be made once per order. Price adjustments are not available on Final Sale items or on items bought during the Black Friday to Cyber Monday sale period.

## Final Sale items
Final Sale items are clearly labeled on the product page. They cannot be returned, and they are not eligible for price adjustments.

## Pre-orders
Pre-order items show an expected ship date on the product page. They are charged when they ship. If the ship date moves back, we email you, and you can cancel the pre-order for a full refund at any time before it ships.

## Price errors
If a product is listed at a clearly wrong price because of a system error, we may cancel the order and refund you in full. We will email you if this happens.
