# BrewNest Privacy and Security Policy

## Information We Collect

- **Account information:** name, email address, shipping address, and phone number
- **Order information:** items purchased, order dates, and delivery details
- **Payment information:** handled by our payment processor. BrewNest never stores your full card number.
- **Usage data:** pages visited, device type, and browser, collected through cookies

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process orders, send shipping updates, provide support, manage subscriptions, and improve our website. With your consent, we also send marketing emails about new coffees and offers.

## Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

- Payment processors, to charge your card
- Carriers (USPS, UPS, Canada Post), to deliver your order
- Email and analytics providers, who work under contract with us
- Authorities, when required by law

## Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep your cart and login working, and analytics cookies to understand site usage. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser or in the cookie banner.

## Marketing Emails

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link at the bottom of any email. Order and shipping emails are still sent because they are required to complete your order.

## Your Rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account. Email privacy@brewnest.example. We respond within 30 days. Some order records must be kept for 7 years for tax purposes.

## Data Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. If you request deletion, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except records we are legally required to keep.

## Account Security

Use a strong password of at least 10 characters. We recommend turning on two-factor authentication in "Account Settings". BrewNest will never ask for your password by email or phone.

## Children

BrewNest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Security Incidents

If a data breach affects your information, we will notify you by email within 72 hours of confirming it.

## Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version. Last updated: January 2025.
