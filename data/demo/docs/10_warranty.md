# Warranty

## Standard warranty
Every Tidewell Home product has a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. The year starts on the delivery date. The warranty belongs to the original purchaser and cannot be transferred, but a gift recipient can make a claim with the order number or the gift receipt.

## Dutch Oven enamel warranty
The Tidewell Enameled Dutch Oven has a longer warranty on its enamel: 10 years from the delivery date. It covers chipping, cracking and crazing of the enamel under normal home use. It does not cover damage from sudden temperature change, impact, metal utensils or steel wool. All other parts of the Dutch Oven, including the lid knob, have the standard 1-year warranty.

## What the warranty covers
It covers defects in materials and workmanship, such as a seam that splits under normal use, a hinge that fails, a zipper that breaks or a coating that peels without being misused.

## What the warranty does not cover
- Normal wear and tear.
- Misuse, accidents, or use that goes against the care instructions, such as washing the Pinebrook knives in a dishwasher.
- Commercial use, such as use in a restaurant or rental business.
- Natural changes in the product: linen shrinking up to 3% or wrinkling, teak weathering to silver-gray, and cedar scent fading.
- Chips on stoneware caused by impact.
- Damage from shipping. For that, see the returns document, which has a 7-day reporting window.

## The 90-day defect rule and the warranty
There are two separate remedies for a defective item. If you report the defect within 90 days of delivery, you can choose a replacement or a refund. If you report it after 90 days but still within the warranty period, we offer a replacement or a repair only, not a refund. We refund only if we cannot replace the item, for example because it is discontinued.

## How to make a warranty claim
Email support@tidewellhome.example with your order number, a short description and clear photos of the problem. We reply within 2 business days. We may ask for more photos. If your claim is approved, we pay the shipping cost for the replacement and, if we need the item back, the return shipping too.

## Warranty and the return fee
A warranty replacement never carries the $6.50 label fee or the 15% restocking fee.

## Replacements that are out of stock
If a replacement is out of stock, we offer a similar item of equal or greater value, or a refund if you do not want it.
