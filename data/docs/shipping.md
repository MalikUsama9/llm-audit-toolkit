# BrewNest Shipping Policy

## Where We Ship

We ship to all 50 US states, including Alaska and Hawaii, and to all Canadian provinces. We do not currently ship to PO Boxes for equipment orders, but coffee-only orders may be sent to PO Boxes using USPS. We do not ship internationally outside the US and Canada.

## Processing Time

- **Coffee orders:** Roasted to order and shipped within 1 business day of roasting. Roasting happens Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Orders placed by 11:59 PM Pacific on the night before a roast day ship that roast day.
- **Equipment orders:** Ship within 2 business days from our warehouse in Reno, Nevada.
- **Mixed orders:** Coffee and equipment may ship separately. You will receive a tracking number for each package.

We do not ship on weekends or on US federal holidays.

## Carriers

We ship with USPS, UPS, and Canada Post. The carrier is chosen automatically based on your address and shipping method. You cannot select a specific carrier.

## Tracking Your Order

You will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number within 24 hours of your order shipping. You can also track orders under "My Orders" in your account. Tracking updates may take up to 12 hours to appear after the first scan.

## Delivery Problems

**Package not received:** If your tracking shows Delivered but you cannot find your package, wait 24 hours and check with neighbors, then contact us. We will open a carrier investigation and, if the package is not found within 7 business days, send a replacement or refund.

**Wrong address:** Please double check your address at checkout. If the address is wrong and the package is returned to us, we can reship for the actual shipping cost. We cannot redirect a package once it has shipped.

**Delays:** During holidays (November 15 to December 31), shipping can take 2 to 3 additional days. Weather and carrier delays are outside our control.

## Shipping Coffee in Hot Weather

Roasted coffee is not affected by summer heat during normal transit. We do not use insulated packaging or ice packs. Please bring packages indoors promptly and do not leave them in a hot car.

## Gift Orders

You can ship to a different address than your billing address and add a free gift message of up to 200 characters. Prices are not shown on the packing slip for gift orders.

## Local Pickup

Local pickup is not available.
