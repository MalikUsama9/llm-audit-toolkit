# Orders and Accounts

## Order confirmation
You receive an order confirmation email right after you pay. If you do not see it within 15 minutes, check your spam folder, then contact support with the email address you used.

## Canceling an order
You can cancel an order yourself, using the Cancel Order button in your account, within 2 hours of placing it. After 2 hours, contact support. We will try to stop the order, but we cannot promise it. Once an order has the status Packed, it can no longer be canceled. You can return it instead when it arrives.

## Changing the shipping address
You can change the shipping address in your account within 2 hours of placing the order. After that, contact support. We can only change the address if the order has not been packed. Once a package has shipped, the address cannot be changed.

## Changing items or quantities
We cannot edit the items or quantities in an order. To change them, cancel the order and place a new one. If the order is past the 2-hour window, contact support.

## Order limits
You can buy up to 10 units of the same item in one order. For larger quantities, contact support. We do not currently have a wholesale or bulk-discount program.

## Orders under review
Some orders are held for a fraud check. A review usually finishes in a few hours and can take up to 48 hours. We email you if we need extra information. If the review fails, we cancel the order and refund the charge in full.

## Order status meanings
- **Received:** we have your order and payment.
- **Packed:** your items are boxed and labeled. The order can no longer be canceled or changed.
- **Shipped:** the carrier has the package and tracking is on its way to your email.
- **Delivered:** the carrier marked the package as delivered. Your 30-day return window starts here.

## Guest checkout and accounts
You can check out as a guest. An account lets you see your order history, start returns, track orders and save addresses. Guests can still start a return by using the order number and the email address from the order.

## Password reset
A password reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If it expires, request a new one. For security we cannot reset a password by phone or email on your behalf.

## Deleting your account
Email support to delete your account. We finish the request within 30 days. We keep order records for 7 years for tax reasons, but we remove the personal details we are not required to keep.

## Gift messages
You can add a gift message of up to 200 characters at checkout. It is printed on the packing slip, and the packing slip shows no prices.
