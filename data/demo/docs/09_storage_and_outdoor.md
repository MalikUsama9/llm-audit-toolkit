# Storage and Outdoor Products

## Cedar Chest
The Cedar Chest is solid eastern red cedar. It measures 40 inches wide, 18 inches deep and 19 inches high, and it weighs 52 lb. It arrives fully assembled. Because it weighs over 40 lb it is a heavy item: it takes 3 business days to process, ships Standard only, is delivered curbside and adds a $19.95 surcharge. It also carries a 15% restocking fee if returned.

## Cedar Chest care
Cedar naturally smells strong at first, and the scent fades over time. To refresh the scent, lightly sand the inside surface with fine sandpaper. Keep the chest away from direct heat and from damp floors. Do not store items that are damp or freshly cleaned inside it, and do not place a chest in direct sun.

## Stackable Wire Baskets
The set of three baskets is powder-coated steel. Each basket holds up to 25 lb. The three baskets stack together to save space. They are for indoor use because the coating can rust when wet.

## Under-Bed Storage Bin
The bin measures 34 by 18 by 6 inches and has a zip lid and clear window. It fits most standard bed frames with at least 7 inches of clearance. The bin is fabric and can be spot cleaned with a damp cloth. It is not waterproof.

## Folding Camp Chair
The Folding Camp Chair has a weight limit of 300 lb. It folds to about 4 inches thick and comes with a carry bag. The frame is powder-coated steel and the seat is polyester. Wipe it clean with mild soap and let it dry fully before packing it away.

## Teak Garden Bench
The bench is 48 inches wide and weighs 61 lb, so it is a heavy item. It ships unassembled. Assembly takes about 30 minutes with the included hex key. Teak is left untreated. Outdoors it weathers to a silver-gray color over time, and you can oil it once a year to keep the golden color. Weathering is natural and is not covered by the warranty. Bring it indoors or cover it in long freezing winters if you can.

## Waterproof Tote (30 L)
The roll-top tote has welded seams and is waterproof against rain and splashes. Do not submerge it or leave it underwater. To stay dry, roll the top at least three times and fasten the buckle. Rinse with fresh water after the beach and let it air dry.

## Outdoor products and the weather
Outdoor products are meant for use outside, but storing them dry in the off-season makes them last longer.
