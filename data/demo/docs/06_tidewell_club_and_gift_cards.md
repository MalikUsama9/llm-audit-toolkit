# Tidewell Club and Gift Cards

## What the Tidewell Club is
The Tidewell Club is our paid membership. It costs $29 per year. The price was $24 per year until December 31, 2024, and became $29 on January 1, 2025.

## Club benefits
- Free Standard shipping on all US orders, including Alaska and Hawaii, with no minimum order. The heavy-item surcharge still applies.
- 5% back in store credit on merchandise. The 5% is calculated on the item price only, not on shipping, tax or gift cards.
- Early access to sales, 24 hours before everyone else.

## When Club store credit arrives
The 5% store credit is added to your account 31 days after your order is delivered, which is just after the 30-day return window closes. If you return an item, you do not get credit for it.

## Joining, renewing and canceling
Membership renews automatically every year. We email a reminder 14 days before renewal. You can cancel any time in your account. If you cancel within 30 days of joining or renewing and have not used any Club benefit, you get a full refund. After that, the fee is not refunded, but you keep the benefits until the end of the paid year.

## Gift cards
Tidewell gift cards come in $25, $50, $100 and $250 amounts. They are delivered by email, normally within 1 hour of purchase. Gift cards cannot be returned or refunded. A gift card cannot be used to buy another gift card. A gift card can be used together with a promo code and with store credit.

## Store credit
Store credit comes from returns (with the 10% bonus if you choose it) and from the Club's 5% back. Store credit never expires. It is applied automatically at checkout, and you can use it on any order.

## Lost gift card emails
If a gift card email does not arrive, check spam, then contact support with the order number. We can resend the code to the same email address. For security we cannot send a code to a different address.
