# Frequently Asked Questions

## Where is my order?
Open your account and go to Orders, or use the tracking link in your shipping email. If tracking has not moved for 7 business days, contact support.

## Do you ship internationally?
We ship to the contiguous US, Alaska, Hawaii and Canada only. Shipping times and prices for each are in the shipping policy.

## What is the cheapest way to get free shipping?
Spend $49 or more in the contiguous US for free Standard shipping. Alaska and Hawaii need $79. Tidewell Club members get free Standard shipping with no minimum.

## Can I return something I bought on sale?
Yes, if it is not marked Final Sale and you return it within 30 days of delivery. Final Sale items cannot be returned.

## How do I exchange an item for a different size?
Start a return for the original item and place a new order for the size you want. If you are exchanging for a different size or color of the same item, there is no return label fee.

## Why was I charged shipping on my large item even though my order was over $49?
Heavy items carry a $19.95 surcharge that free shipping does not remove.

## Can I use two promo codes?
No. One promo code per order.

## Is the Dutch Oven dishwasher safe?
Yes, but we recommend hand washing, because repeated dishwasher cycles can dull the enamel.

## Can I put the Pinebrook knives in the dishwasher?
No. The knives must be hand washed only.

## How do I use my store credit?
It is applied automatically at checkout and does not expire.

## Do you offer gift wrapping?
Please see our gift message options in the orders document.

## How do I change my email address on my account?
Sign in, open Account Settings and update your email. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect when you click it.

## Is my payment information safe?
We do not store your full card number. See the privacy and security document for details.

## What if my package arrives damaged?
Report it within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item and the box, and we send a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

## Who should I contact for a safety concern?
Email safety@tidewellhome.example. We reply within 4 business hours.
