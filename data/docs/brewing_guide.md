# BrewNest Brewing Guide

## The Golden Ratio

A good starting point for most methods is 1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water (1:16). For a strong cup use 1:14, and for a lighter cup use 1:18. Use a digital scale for the best results.

## Water Temperature

Use water between 195 F and 205 F (90 C to 96 C). For light roasts, use the hotter end of the range. For dark roasts, use the cooler end to avoid bitterness.

## Pour-Over (Nest Pour-Over Dripper)

- **Dose:** 20 g coffee, 320 g water
- **Grind:** Medium-fine, like table salt
- **Time:** 3 to 3.5 minutes total

1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water and discard the water.
2. Add the ground coffee and level the bed.
3. Pour 60 g of water and wait 30 seconds (the bloom).
4. Pour the remaining water slowly in circles until you reach 320 g.
5. Let the water drain fully, then remove the dripper and serve.

## French Press (34 oz)

- **Dose:** 56 g coffee, 900 g water
- **Grind:** Coarse, like sea salt
- **Time:** 4 minutes steep

1. Add the coffee and pour in all the water.
2. Stir gently and place the lid on without pressing.
3. Wait 4 minutes, then press the plunger down slowly.
4. Pour right away so the coffee does not over-extract.

## Espresso (Nest Espresso Machine)

- **Dose:** 18 g coffee in, about 36 g espresso out
- **Grind:** Fine
- **Time:** 25 to 30 seconds

Preheat the machine for 15 minutes. If the shot runs too fast and tastes sour, grind finer. If it runs too slow and tastes bitter, grind coarser. We recommend Midnight Espresso for beginners.

## Cold Brew

- **Dose:** 100 g coarse coffee, 800 g cold water
- **Time:** 12 to 16 hours in the refrigerator

Strain through a paper filter. The concentrate keeps for up to 2 weeks in the fridge. Dilute 1:1 with water or milk when serving.

## Grind Size Cheat Sheet

| Method | Grind |
|---|---|
| Espresso | Fine |
| Pour-over | Medium-fine |
| Drip machine | Medium |
| French press | Coarse |
| Cold brew | Extra coarse |

## Troubleshooting

- **Sour or sharp taste:** Under-extracted. Grind finer, use hotter water, or brew longer.
- **Bitter or harsh taste:** Over-extracted. Grind coarser, use cooler water, or brew shorter.
- **Weak coffee:** Use more coffee or a lower water ratio.
- **Muddy cup:** Grind is too fine for the method, or the filter was not rinsed.

## Cleaning Your Equipment

Rinse the dripper and French press after every use. Descale the electric kettle and espresso machine every 2 months with a citric acid solution. Clean grinder burrs every 4 weeks with a dry brush. Never wash grinder burrs with water.
