# Website and Checkout Help

## Supported browsers
Our website works best on the current and previous major versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge. Internet Explorer is not supported. If checkout misbehaves, try a different browser or a private window before contacting support.

## Creating a cart and saving items
Items stay in your cart for 30 days if you are signed in and for 7 days if you are a guest and your browser keeps cookies. Putting an item in a cart does not reserve it. If an item sells out while it is in your cart, it is removed at checkout.

## Wish lists and restock alerts
Signed-in customers can save items to a wish list. On an out-of-stock product page, choose Notify Me and enter your email. We send one email when the item is back. A restock email does not hold the item for you, so popular items can sell out again quickly.

## Applying a promo code
Enter the code in the Promo Code box on the cart page and select Apply. The discount shows before you pay. If a code is rejected, check that the order meets the minimum, that the items are not Final Sale and that you have only one code applied. Codes are not case sensitive.

## Why did my payment fail?
The most common reasons are a mistyped card number, a billing address that does not match the bank's records and a bank block on online purchases. Try again, try another payment method, or ask your bank to approve the charge. A failed payment never creates an order, and we do not keep a hold on your card.

## Why is my total different from the cart?
Shipping and tax are added at checkout. Tax depends on your shipping address. The heavy-item surcharge of $19.95 per heavy item also appears only at checkout, in the shipping line.

## Entering your address
Use a street address for Expedited and Overnight orders. We cannot deliver Expedited or Overnight to a PO box. If your address is flagged by the carrier as incomplete, we may contact you before shipping.

## Signing in with a one-time code
You can sign in with an emailed one-time code instead of a password. The code is valid for 10 minutes and works once.

## Using store credit and gift cards at checkout
Enter the gift card code in the Gift Card box. Store credit is applied automatically if you are signed in. If your balance does not cover the full total, choose a second payment method for the rest. You can use at most 2 payment methods in total.

## Accessibility
We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA on our website. If you find a part of the site that you cannot use, tell support and we will help you place the order by phone or email.

## Reporting a website problem
Email support with the page address, what you expected, what happened, your browser and a screenshot if possible. We do not offer a bug bounty program.
