# BrewNest Returns and Refunds Policy

## Overview

We want you to love your coffee. If you are not satisfied, this policy explains your options. Our return window is 30 days from the delivery date for equipment and 14 days from the delivery date for coffee.

## Coffee Beans and Ground Coffee

**Return window:** 14 days from delivery.

Because coffee is a perishable food product, we cannot accept returns of opened bags. However, we offer our **Fresh Promise**: if you are unhappy with a bag, contact support within 14 days of delivery and we will either send a replacement bag of a different coffee or issue a full refund. You do not need to send the bag back.

Unopened bags can be returned for a full refund if they are sent back within 14 days and are at least 3 weeks away from their best-by date.

## Brewing Equipment

**Return window:** 30 days from delivery.

Equipment includes grinders, kettles, pour-over drippers, espresso machines, scales, and filters. To qualify for a refund, the item must be:

- Unused or lightly used, and in working condition
- In its original packaging with all accessories and manuals
- Accompanied by your order number

Electric items (grinders, kettles, espresso machines) with signs of heavy use, damage, or missing parts may be refused or receive a partial refund of up to 50% of the purchase price.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned

- Opened bags of coffee (see Fresh Promise above)
- Gift cards
- Items marked "Final Sale"
- Custom-engraved products
- Equipment returned after 30 days

## How to Start a Return

1. Log in to your account and go to "My Orders", or email support@brewnest.example with your order number.
2. Select the item and the reason for return.
3. Print the prepaid return label we email to you, or request a QR code for label-free drop-off.
4. Pack the item securely and drop it off with the carrier shown on the label.

Return shipping is **free** for orders within the United States if the item arrived damaged or was sent in error. For all other returns, a flat return shipping fee of $6.95 is deducted from your refund.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to your original payment method. After we receive your return, we inspect it within 3 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days, but your bank may take an additional 3 to 10 days to show the credit.

Original shipping charges are not refunded, except when the return is due to our error or a damaged item.

## Damaged or Wrong Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, email a photo to support@brewnest.example within 7 days of delivery. We will send a replacement at no cost or refund you in full, whichever you prefer. You do not need to return the damaged item if its value is under $40.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. To exchange an item, return the original for a refund and place a new order.

## Warranty

BrewNest brand equipment includes a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Third-party brands follow the manufacturer's own warranty. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, drops, or normal wear such as worn burrs.

## Late or Lost Refunds

If you have not received your refund after 15 business days from approval, contact your bank first, then email us at support@brewnest.example.
