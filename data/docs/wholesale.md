# BrewNest Wholesale Program

## Who Can Apply

The wholesale program is for cafes, restaurants, offices, and retailers who want to buy BrewNest coffee in bulk. You need a valid business license or resale certificate.

## Minimum Order

- First order minimum: $200
- Repeat orders: $150 minimum

## Wholesale Pricing

| Product | Wholesale Price |
|---|---|
| Nest House Blend, 5 lb bag | $52.00 |
| Midnight Espresso, 5 lb bag | $58.00 |
| Sunrise Breakfast Blend, 5 lb bag | $52.00 |
| Single origin coffees, 5 lb bag | $65.00 to $82.00 |

Orders of 50 lb or more receive an additional 5% discount. Wholesale prices are not combined with other promo codes.

## How to Apply

1. Fill out the form at brewnest.example/wholesale.
2. Upload your business license or resale certificate.
3. Our wholesale team reviews your application within 3 business days.
4. Approved accounts receive a login and a free sample box of 4 coffees.

## Ordering and Delivery

Wholesale orders are roasted on the next roast day (Monday, Wednesday, or Friday) and ship within 1 business day. Orders of 25 lb or more ship free in the continental United States. Smaller wholesale orders use standard shipping rates.

## Payment Terms

New accounts pay by credit card at the time of order. After 3 successful orders, you can apply for Net 30 terms, meaning payment is due within 30 days of invoice.

## Private Label

We offer private label roasting with your own bag design for orders of 100 lb or more per roast. Setup costs $350 for custom labels. Lead time is 3 to 4 weeks for the first order.

## Wholesale Returns

Wholesale coffee cannot be returned. If an order is wrong or damaged, email support within 3 days of delivery and we will replace it.
