# Bedding and Bath Products

## Linen Sheet Set
The Linen Sheet Set is 100% European flax linen. It comes in Twin, Full, Queen, King and California King, and in eight colors. Each set has a flat sheet, a fitted sheet and two pillowcases, except Twin, which has one pillowcase. Fitted sheets fit mattresses up to 16 inches deep.

## Linen care
Wash in cold water on a gentle cycle and tumble dry on low. You can also line dry. Do not use bleach. Linen shrinks up to 3% in the first wash, and this is normal. It also gets softer and a little wrinkled with use. The shrinkage and the natural wrinkling are not defects, and they are not covered by the warranty.

## Cloud Duvet Insert
The Cloud Duvet Insert is down alternative with a 350 GSM fill, designed for all seasons. It comes in Twin, Full/Queen, King and California King. It has corner loops to hold a duvet cover. It is machine washable in a large front-loading washer. Do not wash it in a top-loader with a center agitator, because the agitator can tear the baffles. Tumble dry on low with two or three wool dryer balls, and make sure it is fully dry before using it.

## Turkish Cotton Towel Set
The towel set is 100% Turkish cotton at 600 GSM. A 6-piece set has 2 bath towels, 2 hand towels and 2 washcloths. A bath towel measures 30 by 56 inches, a hand towel 16 by 28 inches and a washcloth 13 by 13 inches. Colors are White, Fog and Slate.

## Towel care
Wash new towels once before first use, in warm water with no fabric softener. Softener coats the fibers and makes towels less absorbent. Tumble dry on medium. It is normal for new towels to shed some lint during the first few washes.

## Bath mat
The Tidewell Bath Mat is 21 by 34 inches, cotton, with a non-slip backing. It is machine washable. Do not tumble dry it on high heat because the backing can crack, so use low heat or air dry.

## Bath items and returns
Opened soaps, bath bombs and bath salts cannot be returned. Towels, mats and bedding can be returned within 30 days if they are unwashed and unused.
