# Shipping Policy

## Processing time
Orders placed before 2:00 PM PT on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM PT, or on a weekend or holiday, ship on the next business day. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding company holidays. Heavy items (see below) take 3 business days to process.

## Shipping options and prices for the contiguous US
- **Standard:** 5 to 7 business days, $5.95.
- **Expedited:** 2 to 3 business days, $12.95.
- **Overnight:** next business day, $24.95. Overnight orders must be placed before 12:00 noon PT. Overnight is not available for PO boxes, Alaska or Hawaii.
Delivery times count from the day the order ships, not the day it is placed. PO boxes can only receive Standard shipping.

## Free shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders of $49 or more to the contiguous US. The $49 is measured after discounts and promo codes are applied and before tax. Free shipping applies to Standard only. If you choose Expedited or Overnight on a qualifying order, you pay the full price of that option. Tidewell Club members get free Standard shipping on all US orders with no minimum.

## Alaska and Hawaii
Orders to Alaska and Hawaii ship Standard only. Delivery takes 8 to 12 business days and costs $9.95. Free shipping starts at $79 for these two states.

## Canada
We ship to Canada only. Delivery takes 8 to 14 business days and costs a flat $14.95. There is no free-shipping threshold for Canada. Canadian customers pay any import duties and taxes charged at the border. We do not ship to any other country.

## Heavy items
Items over 40 lb are heavy items. Today these are the Cedar Chest (52 lb) and the Teak Garden Bench (61 lb). Heavy items ship by Standard only and are delivered curbside, which means the driver leaves the item at the curb or the front door and does not carry it inside. Each heavy item adds a $19.95 heavy-item surcharge. The surcharge is charged even when the order qualifies for free shipping, and Tidewell Club membership does not waive it.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed within 24 hours after your order ships. We choose the carrier (UPS, USPS or FedEx), and you cannot request a specific carrier. An order can ship in more than one package. If it does, shipping is charged once.

## Signature on delivery
Orders with a total of $300 or more require a signature on delivery. If nobody can sign, the carrier will try again the next business day and then hold the package at a local facility.

## Holiday season
From November 1 to December 24, processing can take up to 2 extra business days. To arrive by December 24, order by these dates: Standard by December 10, Expedited by December 17, and Overnight by December 22 before 12:00 noon PT. We do not guarantee Christmas delivery for orders placed after those dates.

## Lost packages
If tracking shows no movement for 7 business days, contact support. We open an investigation with the carrier, which takes up to 10 business days. When it finishes, we either send a replacement or refund the order, and you choose which.
